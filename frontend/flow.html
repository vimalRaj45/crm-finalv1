How Salesforge System Operates

Explore the 8 core operational steps powering your automated CRM pipeline. Click on any card below to inspect rules or click Run Flow Simulation!

01

Email Ingestion & De-duplication

IMAP

Backend connects to Gmail via secure IMAP. Normalizes subject lines and groups candidates to process 1 email per batch.

02

Stage 1: Job Extraction

Mistral Small

Extract R&D/Innovation leadership roles using Mistral Small LLM. Strictly exclude Pricing, Sales, Patents, and Operations.

03

Search & URL Prioritization

Serper API

Rank posting URLs strictly: LinkedIn Job > Naukri > Indeed > BeBee > SimplyHired > Official Page.

04

Playwright Scraping & 7-Day Caching

Playwright

Scrape full job description. Consult persistent 7-day cache file to verify company employee size.

05

Stage 2: Job Evaluation & Scoring

Mistral LLM

Mistral classifies role brackets (0-100). System code adds Strategy boost (+15) or Pricing penalty (-20).

06

Threshold Decisions & Qualification

Validation

Qualify only if LinkedIn employees ≥ 500 (unless score ≥ 80 when missing) AND score ≥ 50.

07

CRM Conversion & Deals Board Sync

PostgreSQL

Automatically creates Account, Contact, and Deal record in Lead (20%) stage on the Deals Board.

08

Outreach Sequence Engine & Reply Scanner

Outreach & AI

Dispatches editable Day 0 email, auto-moves Deal card to Contacted (40%), runs Day 3 & Day 7 follow-ups, and scans inbox for customer replies.
